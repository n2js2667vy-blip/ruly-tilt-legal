Quick tips

  • The game requires a device with motion sensors (not available on all Simulator setups).
  • Sign in to Game Center to submit scores and view Global / Friends leaderboards.
  • Sound and haptics can be toggled in the in-app Settings.
  • After a run ends, you can watch a rewarded ad to Continue, or Restart (an interstitial ad may show).
